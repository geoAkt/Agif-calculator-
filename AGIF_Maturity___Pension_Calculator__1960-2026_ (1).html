AGIF Scheme Rules & Timeline (1960–2026)

1. Service Prior to 1976 (1960–1975 Joiners)

Prior to 01 January 1976, there was no Army Group Insurance Scheme. Personnel commissioned or enrolled between 1960 and 1975 served under the original military terms. On 01 January 1976, when AGIF was formally inaugurated, all serving personnel were automatically enrolled and monthly deductions commenced.

3. Savings vs Risk Elements

Roughly ~83% to 85% of each subscription is placed in the member's savings corpus which earns compounding interest. The remaining ~15% to 17% is the non-refundable risk premium that provides the life insurance cover.
